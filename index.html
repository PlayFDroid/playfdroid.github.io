How to Add to F-Droid

Add the repository to your favorite F-Droid client on your Android device.

Method 1: One-Click (From Device)

If you have F-Droid, Droid-ify, or Neo Store installed, tap below:

➕ Open in F-Droid Client

Method 2: Manual Add in Settings

  1. Open F-Droid ➔ Settings ➔ Repositories ➔ tap + (Add).
  2. Paste the Repository URL:
    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/PlayFDroid/fdroid-repo/gh-pages/fdroid/repo
  3. Paste the Signing Fingerprint:
    077873B95749B78FA46971CC117F6F6086639D785208F0DDF3DA2143CB1E8DDD
Scan with camera or in F-Droid
